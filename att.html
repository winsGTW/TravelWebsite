What to Expect at Universal Studios Singapore

Seven immersive themed zones

Step into movie-inspired worlds like Ancient Egypt with giant pyramids and mummies, Sci-Fi City with futuristic skyscrapers, and Far Far Away Castle straight out of Shrek.

World-class rides for all ages

Ride dueling roller coasters on Battlestar Galactica, plunge into darkness on Revenge of the Mummy, cool off on Jurassic Park Rapids Adventure, or enjoy kid-friendly rides in Minion Land and Madagascar.

Spectacular shows and performances

Catch WaterWorld with jaw-dropping stunts, watch Elmo’s TV Time in Hollywood, and enjoy seasonal parades and character dance-offs that fill the streets with energy.

Meet iconic characters up close

Snap selfies with Transformers’ Optimus Prime and Bumblebee, strike a pose with the Minions, or wave to Puss in Boots, Sesame Street friends, and the cast of Madagascar.

Dining and shopping themed to the movies

Dine on turkey legs or themed desserts across the zones, and shop for Minion plushies, Jurassic Park gear, and exclusive Transformers collectibles only sold in the park.
